## Information we collect

If the future production enquiry form is enabled, it is planned to collect a name, telephone number,
optional email address, selected service, project details, source page, privacy consent and limited
technical anti-spam information. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information. Telephone,
email and WhatsApp enquiries are also handled by the services used for those channels.

## Why we use it

We use enquiry information to understand and respond to requests, prepare quotations, discuss and
manage requested services, protect the form from abuse, maintain necessary business records, resolve
disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. The exact governing-law/legal-basis wording remains
subject to professional review.

## Service providers and sharing

The planned website may use its hosting provider, a final owner-approved email/SMTP provider and
Cloudflare Turnstile for form abuse prevention. Enquiries should be delivered to email only; no CRM
was discovered or requested. Existing Google Analytics may be used on the future production site
only after the correct logged-in account/property and consent approach are verified.

Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, Bing scripts and advertising cookies are not
planned. Final provider names, processing locations, transfers and safeguards must be confirmed
before publication. We do not state that personal information is sold.

## Retention

Enquiry information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, prepare
quotations, manage requested services, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes and
meet applicable legal obligations. Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted.

RK Reno Solution should conduct an internal annual review of stored enquiries to identify information
that is no longer required. This review is an internal governance control, not a fixed 12-month
deletion promise and not permission to retain information indefinitely.

## Security

The future production form is planned to use HTTPS, server-side validation, an allowed-origin policy,
a small request-size limit, rate limiting, a honeypot and Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile credentials,
the final SMTP provider and final sender remain unresolved. No internet service can guarantee
absolute security.

## Your choices and requests

Contact rkrenosolution@gmail.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of enquiry information,
subject to applicable law. Identity checks, response timing and regulator/complaint wording remain
subject to professional review.

## Cookies and analytics

Essential website and anti-abuse functions may use limited technical storage. GitHub Pages staging
uses no analytics and remains noindex, nofollow. Non-essential production analytics must remain off
until the approved consent position is implemented. See the draft Cookie and Tracking Notice.
