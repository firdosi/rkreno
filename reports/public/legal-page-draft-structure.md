# Legal page draft structure

These are drafting structures, not final legal text. Bracketed items require owner information and,
where appropriate, qualified legal review. The routes must not be added until the content is
completed and approved.

## Privacy Policy

1. **Who operates the website**
   - [Legal operator name], [registration number], [address], [privacy contact].
2. **Information collected**
   - Name, phone, optional email, requested service, project details, page URL, security and
     submission metadata.
3. **Why information is used**
   - Responding to enquiries, preparing discussions/quotations, preventing spam and maintaining
     service security.
4. **Consent or other processing basis**
   - [Owner/legal reviewer to confirm].
5. **Recipients and processors**
   - Hosting, SMTP/email, Cloudflare Turnstile, CRM if used, analytics/advertising only if approved.
6. **Retention**
   - [Approved enquiry-email and CRM retention periods].
7. **International processing**
   - [Provider locations, transfers and safeguards].
8. **Access, correction and deletion**
   - Request method, identity checks, response contact and complaint route.
9. **Security**
   - High-level safeguards without claiming absolute security.
10. **Updates**
    - Effective date and material-change process.

## Terms of Use

1. Operator and website scope.
2. Informational purpose and permitted use.
3. Enquiries are not accepted quotations or contracts.
4. Scope, price and timing depend on inspection and an approved written quotation.
5. No warranty or guarantee unless explicitly included in an approved contract.
6. Technical information is general and must be assessed for the actual property.
7. Intellectual-property ownership and permitted reuse.
8. Third-party links and provider limitations.
9. Accuracy, availability and liability wording approved by a legal reviewer.
10. Governing law, dispute contact and effective date.

## Cookie and tracking notice, if required

1. Essential storage and security tools.
2. Cloudflare Turnstile processing.
3. Google tag/GA4 only if confirmed and approved.
4. GTM and Meta Pixel only if selected.
5. Purpose and duration of each cookie or storage item.
6. Consent categories: necessary, analytics and advertising.
7. Accept, reject and manage choices where required.
8. How to withdraw consent.
9. Link to the Privacy Policy and contact.

Non-essential tracking must remain disabled until the owner chooses the tools and confirms the
applicable consent requirements.
