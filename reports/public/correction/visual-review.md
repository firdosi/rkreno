# Visual review

## Result

PASS. Automated desktop and mobile review covered all eight required routes at 1440px and 390px. Manual browser review confirmed that the homepage, service, article and blog presentations are visually distinct, customer-facing and usable at both breakpoints.

- Professional quality: cohesive navy, orange, white and light-grey design with consistent controls, radii and spacing.
- Page-type suitability: dedicated landing, directory, contact, FAQ, archive, service and editorial treatments are visible.
- Visual rhythm: alternating open, media, list, table, FAQ and CTA sections avoid a repeated-box layout.
- Readability: long article titles use a reduced scale; editorial bodies use a narrower reading measure.
- Image use: source-purpose imagery is retained, with local route-relevant imagery used when locked remote files are unavailable.
- CTA clarity: WhatsApp and quotation actions remain prominent and stack at 390px.
- Mobile usability: 0px horizontal overflow, no broken images, stacked actions and one-column cards.
- Console result: 0 errors.
